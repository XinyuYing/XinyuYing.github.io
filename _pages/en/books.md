    <h2><em>AI Transformation in China</em></h2>
    <p class="book-feature__byline">Yinliang (Ricky) Tan and Luis (Geng) Liu</p>
    <dl class="book-facts">
      <div><dt>Publisher</dt><dd>Tsinghua University Press</dd></div>

    <p>This Chinese-language book presents nine decision-focused cases from companies operating in retail, healthcare, technology, cross-border commerce, customer service, beverages, education, market research, and digital platforms.</p>
    <p>Rather than treating AI transformation as a technology checklist, the cases examine the strategic choices behind value creation: whether to extend existing capabilities or redesign processes, how to balance short-term efficiency with long-term organizational capability, and where governance and ethical boundaries belong.</p>

    <h2><em>Smart Rivals: How Innovative Companies Play Games That Tech Giants Can't Win</em></h2>
    <p class="book-feature__byline">Feng Zhu and Bonnie Yining Cao · Chinese edition adapted and translated by Yinliang (Ricky) Tan</p>
    <dl class="book-facts">
      <div><dt>Publisher</dt><dd>Publishing House of Electronics Industry</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Published</dt><dd>January 2026</dd></div>
      <div><dt>ISBN</dt><dd>978-7-121-51721-1</dd></div>
    </dl>
    <p>Traditional companies do not have to imitate technology giants. The book offers a more practical path: identify capabilities, customer relationships, and data that are genuinely distinctive, then use digital technology to amplify those advantages.</p>
    <p>Its six-part playbook covers strengthening existing advantages, deepening customer centricity, finding platform opportunities, building ecosystems, managing cooperation and competition, and responding to disruption.</p>
