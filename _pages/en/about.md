<div class="bio-text">
  Dr. Yinliang (Ricky) Tan is Professor of Decision Sciences and Management Information Systems at China Europe International Business School (CEIBS). His research examines the economics and management of artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on how AI affects economic development and how companies navigate AI transformation and organizational change. He has served as a visiting scholar at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (Stanford HAI) and the Stanford Digital Economy Lab.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  Prior to joining CEIBS, Professor Tan was a tenured Professor of Decision and Information Sciences, Bauer Chair Professor, and Director of the DBA Program at the C.T. Bauer College of Business, University of Houston. Before that, he served as Assistant Professor and Associate Professor of Management Science at the A.B. Freeman School of Business, Tulane University, where he also served as Executive Director of the Goldring Institute of International Business. He earned tenure and held an endowed professorship at Tulane.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  Professor Tan received his Ph.D. from Warrington College of Business at the University of Florida, specializing in Information Systems and Operations Management. His research covers artificial intelligence and the digital economy, generative AI and large language model applications, and the managerial mechanisms underlying enterprise AI transformation. He has extensive experience working with and conducting collaborative research with companies in both China and the United States. He also has substantial teaching experience in data analytics and was named Teacher of the Year at the Freeman School of Business.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  Professor Tan has published more than 20 articles in journals included in the <em>Financial Times</em> 50 list of leading academic journals in business, including <em>Management Science</em>, <em>MIS Quarterly</em>, <em>Information Systems Research</em>, <em>Production and Operations Management</em>, and <em>Manufacturing &amp; Service Operations Management</em>. His research has also received multiple best paper awards.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  As of August 2026, Professor Tan is ranked No. 13 worldwide on the Global OM/SCM Faculty Productivity Leaderboard maintained at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. He is also ranked No. 1 among scholars based at institutions in mainland China. The ranking is based on publications in UTD24 journals and is adjusted for the number of years since completion of the Ph.D. In 2026, Professor Tan was included for the first time in the World’s Top 2% Scientists list, developed by researchers at Stanford University in collaboration with Elsevier. Based on Scopus data, the list uses citation counts, the h-index, the co-authorship-adjusted hm-index, and a composite citation indicator to assess researchers’ scholarly impact.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  Professor Tan currently serves as a Senior Editor of <em>Production and Operations Management</em>; an Associate Editor of <em>Information Systems Research</em>, <em>Decision Support Systems</em>, and <em>Information &amp; Management</em>; and a Department Editor of <em>Decision Sciences</em>. He also serves on the editorial boards of the <em>Journal of Operations Management</em>, the <em>Journal of the Association for Information Systems</em>, and the <em>Journal of Management Information Systems</em>. In addition, he is a judge for the Harvard Business Review China “Dingge Awards” and a member of the Standardization Committee of the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Technology Association.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  In 2019, Professor Tan was named one of Poets&amp;Quants’ Top 40 Business School Professors Under 40 and received an Early Career Achievement Award from the Decision Sciences Institute. In 2022, he received the Sandra A. Slaughter Early Career Award from the Information Systems Society in recognition of his contributions to the information systems field. In 2025, he received the CEIBS Annual Best Research Award and the Best-Case Development Award in the artificial intelligence category from The Case Centre. In 2026, he won First Prize in the 2026 China Business Case Teaching Competition and the 8th Shanghai MBA Case Teaching Competition. In October 2026, <em>POOK Technology: How AI Drives Business Value and Enhances Productivity</em>, co-authored by Professor Tan and case writer Geng Liu, was named one of the <em>Financial Times</em>’ top 15 bestselling new business school cases worldwide.
</div>

<div class="bio-text">
  In his spare time, Professor Tan enjoys collecting works of art and manuscripts by notable historical figures. He also maintains a strong interest in the intersection of art, history, and technology.
</div>
